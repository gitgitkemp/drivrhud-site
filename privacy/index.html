DRIVR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Drivr is a speedometer app for car and motorbike. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. We, the makers, do not collect, sell or share your personal data. A few features use outside services, described below.

Location and motion

Drivr uses your location for your speed, heading, trips and parking spot, and the motion sensor for the G-meter and lean angle. This is processed on your phone only. Your trips and parking spots are stored on your phone and are removed when you delete the app. Exporting a trip only happens when you choose to.

Speed limits (Drivr Pro)

To show the speed limit, Drivr downloads map tiles for the area around you from our tile server (hosted on GitHub Pages). A tile covers several square kilometres, so the request reveals the rough area, not your exact position. If a tile is unavailable, Drivr may ask a public OpenStreetMap service (Overpass API) for the roads around your position. Like any web server, these services can see your IP address. We do not receive or keep this information, and it is not linked to you.

Map, search and navigation (Mapbox)

The live map, address search and route directions are provided by Mapbox. To draw the map and plan a route, your position, the place you search for and your destination are sent to Mapbox. By default the Mapbox map also sends anonymous usage and location data to Mapbox to improve its maps and travel times. This data is not linked to you or used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → More → Privacy ("Help Mapbox improve the map") or via the (i) button on the map. See the Mapbox privacy policy.

Street names and trip maps (Apple)

The street name and the maps of past trips use Apple services (Apple Maps and geocoding), under Apple's privacy policy.

Competition with friends (Drivr Pro, optional)

Only if you join the competition, Drivr stores the name you choose and your monthly and all-time totals (distance, highest G-force, top speed) in Apple's iCloud, so the friends you add can see them. No routes or locations are shared. If you allow notifications, iCloud sends you a message when a friend overtakes you. Leaving the competition in the app deletes your entry.

Purchases

Drivr Pro is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have Pro.

Feedback

If you send us an email from the app, we receive what you write, your email address and the device details shown in the email (app version, iPhone model, iOS version). We use this only to reply and improve Drivr, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Your trips and records stay on your phone; delete the app to remove them. To remove your competition entry, choose “Leave the competition” in the app. Questions or requests under the GDPR? Email support@drivrhud.com.

Children

Drivr is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.

Privacybeleid

Drivr heeft geen account, geen advertenties en geen tracking. Wij als makers verzamelen, verkopen of delen geen persoonsgegevens.

Locatie en beweging worden alleen op je telefoon verwerkt, voor je snelheid, koers, ritten, parkeerplek, G-meter en hellingshoek. Ritten en parkeerplekken staan op je telefoon en verdwijnen als je de app verwijdert.

Snelheidslimieten (Pro): Drivr haalt kaarttegels van je omgeving op bij onze tegelserver (GitHub Pages). Een tegel beslaat enkele vierkante kilometers, dus alleen het globale gebied is zichtbaar. Lukt dat niet, dan kan Drivr een openbare OpenStreetMap-dienst (Overpass API) vragen naar de wegen rond je positie. Zoals elke webserver zien deze diensten je IP-adres. Wij ontvangen of bewaren dit niet.

Kaart, zoeken en navigatie komen van Mapbox. Daarvoor gaan je positie, je zoekopdracht en je bestemming naar Mapbox. De kaart stuurt standaard ook anonieme gebruiks- en locatiegegevens naar Mapbox om kaarten te verbeteren, niet gekoppeld aan jou. Dit zet je uit via Instellingen → Meer → Privacy of de (i)-knop op de kaart.

Competitie (Pro, vrijwillig): alleen als je meedoet, staan je gekozen naam en je totalen (afstand, G-kracht, topsnelheid) in iCloud, zodat je vrienden ze zien. Geen routes of locaties. Stoppen met de competitie verwijdert je gegevens.

Straatnamen en ritkaarten gebruiken diensten van Apple. Aankopen lopen via Apple; wij zien geen betaalgegevens.

Feedback per mail gebruiken we alleen om te antwoorden en Drivr te verbeteren.

Vragen of verzoeken onder de AVG: support@drivrhud.com.
